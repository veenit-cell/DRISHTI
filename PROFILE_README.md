## What I'm Building

### 🚨 [DRISHTI / RescueOps](https://github.com/veenit-cell/DRISHTI)

A human-supervised disaster-operations platform that turns incomplete field evidence into accountable, commander-approved action. It combines evidence review, coverage gaps, route and capability constraints, mission workflows, offline reconciliation, and operational auditability.

### 🌱 [CarbonLoop](https://github.com/veenit-cell/CARBON-LOOP-FINAL)

A gamified sustainability platform that turns low-carbon activities into missions, measurable progress, leaderboards, and rewards. Its MVP uses a deterministic, versioned carbon engine rather than an LLM for official calculations; demo activity data and rewards are explicitly synthetic or mocked.
